5 FACTS ABOUT INDONESIA

Largest Archipelago

Indonesia is the world's largest island country, consisting of over 17,000 islands across the equator.

Home of Komodo Dragons

Indonesia is the only place on Earth where you can find Komodo dragons living in the wild.

Rich Cultural Diversity

There are more than 700 living languages and hundreds of distinct ethnic groups across the nation.

Vat Rainforests

Indonesia hosts the third-largest tropical rainforest in the world, rich in incredible biodiversity.

Ring of Fire

Located on the Pacific Ring of Fire, Indonesia is home to more than 120 active volcanoes.
